# Illustration

Load this when a direction would say more with an image than with text alone.

## Where it earns its place

- **Yes:** empty states, onboarding, success and completion, an error that needs warmth, a
  marketing section, and any moment where a picture carries a state or an idea better than
  words — the rare moments where the delight budget lives (`motion.md` §3).
- **No:** dense working screens where the person reads or acts on data.

## Picture or geometry: the context decides

Neither genre is preferred. A picture — a scene, a figure, an object — can carry a state or a
thought far better than shapes, and shapes can be dull and forgettable. Geometry — lines,
circles, nodes, a diagram, linework that draws itself — is exact and easy to make well, and
often the clearest way to show a process or a status. Choose by what the moment has to say, in
the product's illustration style when it has one.

## The bar

Whatever the genre, the image is good, or it does not ship. A badly drawn person, object or
scene reads as amateur and drags the whole direction down; the critic rejects it
(`critic.md`). Ways to reach the bar:

- **An image generator,** when this session has one, with a short prompt: the subject, the
  product's palette and style, the frame and its size, what to avoid.
- **A style you can execute exactly:** flat shapes, a limited palette, a clear silhouette, a
  consistent stroke — pictures built from forms you can specify, not imitated brushwork or
  shading.
- **Variants:** offer two or three illustration takes for the chosen direction and let the
  person pick.
- **An honest slot:** when none of these reaches the bar, keep the image's place at its real
  size with a one-line art direction, and say what it should become (`craft-floor.md`, Media
  role). Never fill it with a weak fake, never collapse the direction into text.

## It ships as drawn

The image on the canvas is the asset that ships, not a sketch to be redrawn later. Export it at
its real size (SVG, or a raster at least twice its displayed size), with its colours from the
product's tokens, and say in the decision where it lives.
